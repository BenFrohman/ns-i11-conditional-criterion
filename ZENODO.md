## Description (paste as the abstract)

Working preprint. Does NOT claim the Clay statement.

This note records (i) the Biot–Savart identification of the strain kernel A(θ,ω) = (θ × ω) ⊗ θ + θ ⊗ (θ × ω), (ii) the prefactor lock κ = C_CZ(3) = (3/8π)·4π = 3/2 ≠ 1, (iii) same-point cancellation and the |sin φ| pairing, and (iv) an official *conditional* regularity criterion: if a data-only or energy-controlled bound on ||S||_∞ exists in a space that controls the Beale–Kato–Majda integral, then the classical solution on T³ exists globally. Named gaps T1–T10 record why that bound is not derived here. An L^∞ bound on S on the whole torus is emptiness of the singular set, not P¹-nullity (Caffarelli–Kohn–Nirenberg). The two-point strain pairing that would discharge Constantin–Fefferman is not free.
